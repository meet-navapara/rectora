Rectora privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026. This policy describes the Rectora Android app as it works today.

Who this applies to

Rectora can be played as a guest or with a Google account. An internet connection is required before play starts. The app does not ask you to state your age.

Information stored on the device

  • A display name and avatar you choose.
  • Puzzle progress, drafts, statistics, tutorial status, and daily and weekly dates.
  • Settings such as theme, sound, haptics, and timer.
  • If you sign in, the Google account id, and the name and email Google returns. The sign-in token is kept in memory for that session and is not written into the save.

Guest progress and each Google account’s progress are stored separately on the device. Android backup may include the local save. That backup is controlled by the device account, not by a Rectora server.

Google sign-in and Firebase

Continue with Google sends the sign-in to Google and then to Firebase Authentication in the Firebase project com-rectora-rectora. Rectora uses that sign-in to recognize the same player. Cancelling sign-in leaves the guest save unchanged.

After a successful sign-in, puzzle progress can be stored in Cloud Firestore at users/{Firebase user id}, including a progress document under that user. Firestore rules allow that user to read and write only their own documents. Rectora does not ship a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Deleting your account

Delete account in Settings permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication user, your Firestore progress, and the progress saved for that account on the device. It does not delete your Google account. Guest progress on the device is kept. Other ways to request deletion.

Clearing local progress, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app removes the save stored on the device. Those actions do not by themselves delete your account.

Advertising

Rectora can show ads from Google AdMob. The ordinary hint stays free. An optional extra hint can show a rewarded ad, and the hint is added only after Google reports that the reward was earned. After several completed levels, a full-screen ad may appear. Banner ads can appear on chapter and calendar screens, not on puzzle boards. Lessons do not show that ad. If an ad cannot load, play continues.

When an ad request runs, Google’s ad SDK can collect device identifiers, including the advertising ID, and advertising information under Google’s own policy. Where the law requires it, Rectora asks for consent first, and Settings lets you change that choice.

Children

Rectora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Permissions

The app requests internet access so the connection check, Google sign-in, Firebase, and ad requests can run. It does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or SMS.

Contact

Use the support address on the Google Play listing for this app. This policy does not invent a private email address.
