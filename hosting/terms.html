Rectora terms and conditions

Effective 30 October 2026. These terms apply to the Rectora Android app.

The game

Rectora is a puzzle game with Parcels, Trace, Inks, and Threads. You may play as a guest or sign in with Google. An internet connection is required to open the game. Guest progress stays on the device. A Google sign-in can store a progress record in Firebase for that login.

Your account

If you use Google sign-in, you must follow Google’s account rules. Rectora may refuse a sign-in that Google or Firebase rejects. Delete account in Settings permanently removes your Rectora account and its progress. It does not delete your Google account.

Acceptable use

Do not break, overload, or misuse the sign-in or Firebase service, and do not try to read another player’s cloud record. Puzzle content in the app is for play inside Rectora.

Ads

Rectora can show ads from Google AdMob. A rewarded ad grants an extra hint only when Google reports the reward. Play continues if an ad does not load.

Availability

The game and the cloud record are provided as they are. Progress can be lost if the device is cleared, the app is removed, or the cloud write fails. There is no promise that a particular level, reward, or sync will always be available.
