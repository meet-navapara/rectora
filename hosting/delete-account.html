Delete your Rectora account

This page explains how to delete a Rectora account, which exists only if you chose to sign in to the Rectora Android app with Google. Guest players have no account.

In the app

  1. Open Rectora and sign in with the Google account you used before.
  2. Open Settings from the home screen.
  3. Under Your data, choose Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email the support address on the Rectora Google Play listing from the Google account you used. Ask for your Rectora account to be deleted. The request is completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication user.
  • Your progress record in Cloud Firestore, including solved puzzles, statistics, and settings.
  • The progress saved for that account on the device, when you delete from the app.

What is kept

Nothing about your account is kept by Rectora after deletion. Your Google account is separate and is not affected. Guest progress on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Device backups are controlled by your device account.
